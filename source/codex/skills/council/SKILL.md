# base_project:managed
name: council
description: Pressure-test a consequential decision through five independent perspectives and a synthesis pass. Use only when the user invokes $council or explicitly asks for this multi-pass review.
---

Always confirm before running. Explain in the user's language that the council uses roughly six reasoning passes and therefore costs materially more tokens than a direct answer. If the decision is simple or low-stakes, recommend the direct answer. Continue only after a clear yes.

1. Rewrite the decision as a neutral, self-contained prompt without leaning toward an answer.
2. Produce five independent analyses that do not rely on each other's conclusions:
   - Skeptic: flaws, missing evidence, and optimism bias.
   - Pragmatist: time, cost, maintenance, and reversibility.
   - Advocate: impact on the most affected stakeholder.
   - Contrarian: strongest honest case against the obvious answer.
   - Domain Expert: deepest relevant technical or subject-matter analysis.
3. In a sixth “President” pass, compare all five, identify agreement and disagreement, and choose one recommendation. State what evidence would change it when close.
4. Report the President's verdict first, followed by one concise line per advisor.
